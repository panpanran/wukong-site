Wukong: The Sixth Journey

西游记题材的英雄塔防 · A Journey to the West hero tower-defense game

操控悟空、八戒、唐僧、沙僧和小白龙，一边亲自上阵，一边布塔守关，从五行山一路打到西天。 支持单人和本地多人，Boss 战有专属技能和剧情演出。

Play as Wukong, Bajie, Tang Seng, Sha Seng and the White Dragon Horse. Fight on the front line, build towers, and journey west from the Five Elements Mountain. Single player and local co-op, with hand-crafted boss fights and story cutscenes.

删除账号和数据

适用于《Wukong: The Sixth Journey》（Ran Pan） · English version below

在游戏里直接删除（最快）

登录后打开 账户 → 删除账号，再点一次确认，账号和下面列出的全部数据会立即删除。

或者发邮件申请

  1. 用你注册账号的邮箱发邮件到 panpanr.dev@gmail.com，标题写“删除账号”。
  2. 邮件里写上你的游戏昵称（如果记得）。为了确认是你本人，请务必用注册邮箱发送；用 Google 登录的，请用那个 Google 邮箱。
  3. 我们会在 30 天内完成删除，并回信告知。

会删除的数据

  • 账号本身：邮箱地址、账号 ID、昵称
  • 全部游戏数据：等级经验、关卡进度、对局战绩、道具、天赋、成就

删除后无法恢复。我们不会保留上述任何数据。

不会删除的数据

  • Google Play 的购买记录由 Google 保存，不在我们这里，删除账号不会影响你已购买的内容在 Google Play 上的记录。
  • 游客模式的进度只在你的设备上，卸载游戏即可清除。

Delete your account and data

For Wukong: The Sixth Journey by Ran Pan

Delete in the game (fastest)

Sign in, open Account → Delete account and tap again to confirm. Your account and all the data listed below are deleted immediately.

Or request deletion by email

  1. Email panpanr.dev@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my account”.
  2. Include your in-game display name if you remember it. To confirm it is you, please send from your account email; if you use Google Sign-In, send from that Google address.
  3. We complete the deletion within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account: email address, account ID, display name
  • All game data: level and XP, stage progress, match history, items, talents, achievements

Deletion is permanent. We keep none of the data above.

Data that is not deleted

  • Google Play purchase records are kept by Google, not by us; deleting your account does not affect them.
  • Guest-mode progress is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game.

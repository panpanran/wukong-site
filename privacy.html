隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 3 日 · English version below

本政策说明 Ran Pan（以下称“我们”）开发的游戏《Wukong: The Sixth Journey》（以下称“本游戏”） 会收集哪些信息、如何使用，以及你拥有的权利。

1. 不登录也能玩

本游戏可以以游客身份游玩。游客模式下，游戏进度只保存在你的设备上，我们不会收到任何能识别你身份的信息。

2. 登录账号时我们收集的信息

如果你选择注册或登录账号（邮箱密码，或 Google 登录），我们会保存：

信息用途
邮箱地址、账号 ID登录、找回账号
昵称在游戏内显示
游戏数据：等级经验、关卡进度、对局战绩（关卡、胜负、用时、击杀、伤害、所用英雄）、道具、天赋、成就云端保存进度、显示战绩

使用 Google 登录时，Google 会把你的邮箱和基本资料提供给我们，用于创建账号；我们拿不到你的 Google 密码。

3. 我们不做的事

  • 本游戏没有广告，不使用任何广告或第三方统计 SDK。
  • 不出售你的信息，也不把它用于广告。
  • 不收集位置、通讯录、照片、麦克风等设备信息。

4. 用到的第三方服务

服务作用
Supabase账号登录和云端存档的数据库托管
Cloudflare R2下载游戏模型、音效等资源文件（服务器会像所有网站一样记录请求的 IP 地址等技术日志）
Google Play应用分发；如有购买，由 Google Play 处理付款，我们收不到你的支付信息
ipify仅在使用局域网联机时，查询本机公网 IP 用于连接

所有网络传输都经过 HTTPS 加密。

5. 保存期限和删除

账号数据会保存到你删除账号为止。你可以随时按照 删除账号页面的说明申请删除，我们会在 30 天内删除你的账号及上面列出的全部游戏数据。 游客数据只在你的设备上，卸载游戏即可清除。

6. 儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。如果你认为我们误收了此类信息，请联系我们删除。

7. 政策变更

如本政策有更新，我们会修改本页的生效日期；重大变更会在游戏内提示。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information Ran Pan ("we") collects in the game Wukong: The Sixth Journey (the "Game"), how it is used, and your rights.

1. You can play without an account

The Game can be played as a guest. In guest mode your progress is stored only on your device and we receive no information that identifies you.

2. Information we collect when you sign in

If you choose to create an account or sign in (email and password, or Google Sign-In), we store:

InformationPurpose
Email address, account IDSign-in and account recovery
Display nameShown in the Game
Game data: level and XP, stage progress, match history (stage, win/loss, duration, kills, damage, hero used), items, talents, achievementsCloud saves and match history

With Google Sign-In, Google shares your email address and basic profile with us to create your account. We never receive your Google password.

3. What we do not do

  • The Game has no ads and uses no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • We do not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone or similar device data.

4. Third-party services

ServicePurpose
SupabaseDatabase hosting for accounts and cloud saves
Cloudflare R2Downloading game assets such as models and audio (like any web server, it logs technical data such as IP addresses)
Google PlayApp distribution; any purchases are processed by Google Play and we never receive your payment details
ipifyOnly when using local-network multiplayer: looks up your public IP to connect

All network traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.

5. Retention and deletion

Account data is kept until you delete your account. You can request deletion at any time by following the Delete account page; we delete your account and all game data listed above within 30 days. Guest data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the effective date on this page, and notify you in the Game of significant changes.
